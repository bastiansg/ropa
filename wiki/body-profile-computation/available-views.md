# Available Body Views

Each body provides four full-body photographs. Together they expose the front and side silhouettes in relaxed and extended-arm poses.

## 1. Relaxed front

![Relaxed front view](../../resources/test-bodies/test-01/01-front.png)

- File: `01-front.png`
- Orientation: the body faces the camera directly.
- Pose: standing upright, feet slightly apart, with both arms relaxed beside the torso.
- Available information: the unobstructed front silhouette, shoulder position, and front-facing landmarks.

## 2. Extended-arm front

![Extended-arm front view](../../resources/test-bodies/test-01/02-front.png)

- File: `02-front.png`
- Orientation: the body faces the camera directly.
- Pose: standing upright with both arms extended horizontally.
- Available information: the front torso silhouette without the arms covering its sides, plus the arm and joint landmarks.

## 3. Raised-arms side

![Raised-arms side view](../../resources/test-bodies/test-01/03-side.png)

- File: `03-side.png`
- Orientation: the body is shown in profile.
- Pose: standing upright with both arms extended vertically above the head.
- Available information: the side torso silhouette without the arms covering it.

## 4. Relaxed side

![Relaxed side view](../../resources/test-bodies/test-01/04-side.png)

- File: `04-side.png`
- Orientation: the body is shown in profile.
- Pose: standing upright with both arms relaxed beside the torso.
- Available information: the natural side silhouette, neck profile, and side-facing landmarks.

## Shared capture characteristics

Across all four views, the complete body is visible from head to feet, the camera is approximately level with the body, the background is plain, and close-fitting clothing leaves the silhouette visible. These characteristics describe the currently available photographs; they are not yet validation rules for accepting new inputs.
